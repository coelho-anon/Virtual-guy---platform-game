Overview:

Virtual Guy is a 2D platform game with charming graphics and engaging gameplay. The player takes on the role of a brave character in search of exciting adventures.

Game Instructions:

  • Use the arrow keys on your keyboard to move the character left and right.
  • Press the up arrow to jump over obstacles and reach higher platforms.
  • Collect coins to earn points and increase your score.
  • Avoid enemies and traps to avoid losing lives.
  • Reach the end of each level to advance to the next thrilling challenge.

Features and Functionalities:

Get ready to embark on an exciting journey full of challenges and fun! Check out the incredible features and functionalities that await you in "Virtual Guy":

  • 3 thrilling levels: Venture through three unique worlds, each with intriguing challenges and puzzles to overcome. Face environments filled with dangers and discover what each level holds for your brave character.
  • Scoring system: Feel the adrenaline as you collect shiny coins along your way! With each coin earned, your score increases, and you can challenge your friends to see who achieves the highest score!
  • Stunning graphics: Delight in colorful graphics and stunning backgrounds! Every detail has been carefully crafted to provide a delightful visual experience.
  • Smooth animations: Move gracefully through the game world, thanks to fluid animations that bring your character and the environment to life.
  • Engaging soundtrack: Immerse yourself in an engaging soundtrack that accompanies every step of your adventure. Well-placed sound effects add an extra dose of fun to your journey.
  • One heart only: Your character has only one life, so be careful! Avoid dangerous enemies and challenging obstacles to ensure the adventure continues.
  • Save points: Don't worry about starting over! The game has strategically placed save points so you can resume your journey from where you left off. Stay alert to find these precious checkpoints!

Customization:

The game "Virtual Guy" offers various customization options to make your experience unique and special. Below are some of the customization options available:

  • Graphics: You can customize the game graphics by changing the sprites of characters, enemies, scenarios, and objects. To do this, follow the steps below:
    1. Open the Construct2 software and load the "virtual guy - source.capx" project.
    2. In the Project > objects types panel, select the object whose sprite you want to change.
    3. In the properties panel, click on the "Change Sprite" option and select the image you want to use as the new sprite.
    4. Repeat this process for other graphic elements you want to customize.
    5. Save the project with a new name to avoid accidental data loss.
  • Music and Sound Effects: The game's soundtrack and sound effects can also be customized to fit your desired style and atmosphere. Follow the steps below:
    1. In Construct2, go to the "Project" tab and then "Sounds".
    2. Import your own audio files or sounds to replace the existing ones by left-clicking on the folder where you want to import the files.
    3. In the events panel, adjust the events related to music and sound effects to match your changes.
    4. Save the project to ensure that the changes are applied.
  • Editing Tips
  • Levels and Challenges: The game features three exciting levels, but you can create new levels or modify existing ones to offer different challenges to players. Use Construct2 to create new level layouts, add enemies and traps, and adjust the difficulty according to your preferences.
  • Power-Ups and Items: Customize the game's power-ups and items to make the experience more dynamic and strategic. Create new objects and define their characteristics, such as effects and duration time.
  • Visual Customization: Explore different options to customize the overall appearance of the game. Change colors, add extra animations, or visual effects to create a unique atmosphere for "Virtual Guy".

Remember to carefully test the changes you make to ensure that the game continues to function correctly and that the player's experience remains enjoyable.
